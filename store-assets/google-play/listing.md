## English translation (optional: Store listing → Manage translations → English)

### Full description

```
An independent app for Qassim University students, not affiliated with the university.

Qu Student brings everything a Qassim University student needs for the semester into one simple app.

📅 Build your timetable in minutes
• Import the offered courses straight from the university portal after you sign in.
• Pick sections and watch your weekly timetable take shape.
• Add more than one section of the same course (lecture, lab, tutorial).
• Instant warnings for time clashes and shared final-exam periods.
• Undo any change with one tap.

📝 Timetable summary and exam periods
• Sections, rooms and lecturers on one page.
• The final-exam period of every course.

📤 Export and share
• Image, PDF, Excel file, or print directly.
• A ready-made message for WhatsApp or Telegram.
• Copy section numbers to register them quickly in the portal.
• Add your lectures to your phone's calendar (Google, iPhone or Outlook).

🎓 GPA calculator
• Semester and cumulative GPA on the 5-point or 4-point scale.
• Your timetable courses appear automatically; just pick the grades.

⏰ Absence calculator
• See your absence percentage and how many hours you have left in each course.
• Clear levels: safe, warning, danger.
• Add any course by hand to track its absences.

🗓️ Academic calendar
• Semester start, holidays and exams, with what's happening now and what's next.

⭐ Qeeem
• Browse lecturer reviews and search for your section's lecturer directly.

🏠 Home
• Your next lecture, its time and room, and the latest announcements.

🔒 Privacy first
• No accounts, no ads, no tracking.
• Your timetable, grades and absences stay on your device.
• You sign in on the university's official page; the app never reads or stores your password.

Suggestions and feedback: qu4611@gmail.com
```
